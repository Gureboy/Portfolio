These world sprites are unmodified or frame-cropped from Azure Peak:

- grass.png: icons/turf/grass.png
- sand.png and sand-alt-1.png through sand-alt-3.png: icons/turf/sand.dmi
- water-frames.png: icons/turf/newwater.dmi, state "together"
- stone-floor.png: icons/turf/roguefloor.dmi, state "herringbone"
- stone-wall.png and wood-wall.png: icons/turf/roguewall.dmi, states
  "stonebrick" and "woodplain"
- snow-floor.png: icons/turf/snow.dmi, state "snow"
- pine-tree.png, pine-tree-alt.png, dead-tree.png, and snow-tree.png:
  icons/obj/flora/pines.dmi
- flower-clump.png and rock-pile.png: icons/obj/flora/plants.dmi and
  icons/obj/flora/rocks.dmi
- sakura-tree.png: icons/obj/structures/sakura_tree.dmi
- human-actors.png: directional Carbon Human body frames from
  icons/roguetown/mob/bodies/m/mt.dmi, composited with the
  archaiccuirass, laciedress, sofiavest_f, and eirencoat states from
  icons/clothing/onmob/donor_clothes.dmi; the exposed body pixels are
  colorized for the scene
- human-hair.png: directional hairstyle frames from
  icons/mob/sprite_accessory/hair/human_hair.dmi, tinted for individual
  characters and composited over the Carbon Human sprites
